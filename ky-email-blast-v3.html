For Over Six Decades

Camp Morasha has been more than just a summer experience — it has been a community, a standard of excellence, and a place where generations of children have grown in their love for yiddishkeit and dedication to Eretz Yisrael. Since our founding in 1964, our camp's core values and mission have guided every step forward. Today, we are excited to share a historic and meaningful new chapter in that journey.

Built for Boys

We are incredibly proud to announce Morasha's Kehillas Yaakov (MKY) — our new all-boys full-summer overnight camp that combines strong structure, thoughtful programming, and a warm, growth-oriented environment. MKY is a camp grounded in the belief that a summer in a wholesome environment plays a significant role in shaping a child's relationship with Torah and avodas Hashem, his middos, love for Israel, and relationships with others.

A Staff That Inspires

We know that a camp is only as good as its staff. Therefore, assembling the ideal team is a top priority for MKY. We look forward to announcing our leadership team in August — and hiring highly motivated mechanchim, counselors, athletes, and camping professionals, who come with positive energy, a love for camp, and the recognition that every staff member is a role model to our boys.

A Campus Like No Other

We are thrilled to have secured an exceptional property located just minutes away from Camp Morasha. The campus, surrounded by hundreds of acres of natural beauty, features a wide range of first-rate facilities: A modern Beis Medrash at its center, a Dining Hall that overlooks a scenic mountain landscape, and the most unbelievable and expansive sports and activity spaces. Every element supports both an active, exciting summer and an environment that feels inspiring, uplifting, and mission-driven.

Passionately Professional

MKY will benefit from the same administrative leadership and commitment to excellence that define Morasha. While operationally and programmatically distinct, the two camps will create powerful synergies — shared resources, systems, talent, and expertise — that will elevate both programs. By expanding thoughtfully, we are investing in a future that allows Camp Morasha to continue thriving while impacting more families in meaningful ways.
