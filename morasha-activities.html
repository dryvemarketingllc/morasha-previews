Daily Activities

Our 400-acre property includes a full range of recreational facilities that satisfy the varied athletic, aquatic, artistic, and adventure interests of our campers. We offer a wide array of activities, ranging from intense sports to the best of the fine arts. These experiences encourage campers to discover and develop their individual abilities in ways that connect to their personal interests and lifestyles. Participation in camp-wide activities also gives them the opportunity to express their talents in a group setting.

Overnight and Day Trips

Trips offer unique opportunities for campers to form special bonds with their peers in a different setting. Each trip is crafted down to the smallest detail, ensuring that every camper has an unforgettable experience in a safe and fun environment. Take a moment to see what trips our campers enjoy each summer.

Road Trip

Our oldest campers fly down to Florida and begin a 6-day, 6-state road trip up the East Coast. This trip includes a wide range of fun and inspiring activities while exposing campers to different "walks of life" across the American landscape. Whether it's an awesome day at Universal Studios in Orlando, an inspiring Shabbat in Washington, DC, an intense day with the Marines in South Carolina, or a relaxing day at Virginia Beach, this trip promises to be unforgettable.

NYC Bash

This two-day trip is a non-stop experience of raw excitement for our Manhigim/GOT campers. They've never seen NYC like this—featuring a limo scavenger hunt through the city, an all-night sleepover at the Sportime USA arcade complex, the IMAX experience, Bounce Trampoline Park, and a Golden Movie Ticket. It's unlike anything else.

Lake George Wilderness Trip

This trip is all about the thrill and exhilaration of the outdoors. It's a two-day experience packed with intensity and fun, where our Ilanot campers are challenged at the Adirondack Extreme Adventure Course and on whitewater rafting excursions. The trip also includes a visit to Six Flags in Lake George and an outdoor drive-in movie.
