Morasha Kollel

Investing in Torah Leadership

Morasha Kollel stands among the most serious and respected summer kollelim in the Orthodox community. Each summer, dedicated yeshiva and kollel scholars immerse themselves in sustained, high-level limud Torah within a vibrant and rigorous beis medrash environment.
