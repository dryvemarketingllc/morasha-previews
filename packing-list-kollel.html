Seforim List

Members of the Kollel should keep an eye out for an email with the full Sefarim list. Below are the essentials:

  • A Chumash (Bamidbar/D'varim) with Rashi, for Shnayim Mikra (boys are Ma'avir Sedra before each Shabbos)
  • Meseches Brachos — the High School Kollel will be learning this mesechta
  • Kinos with English translation — for campers in camp for Tisha B'Av (first session). The Kollel has Kinos available, but bringing one with an English translation is highly recommended
  • Mini-Shas — if you own one, you are encouraged to bring it
  • A Jastrow dictionary — highly recommended
  • Any sefarim for Shabbos Divrei Torah preparation — each member of the HS Kollel will be asked to say Divrei Torah once or twice on Shabbosim throughout the summer

The Kollel has a full Beis Medrash with seforim for everyone's use. You are welcome to bring additional sefarim you plan to learn on Shabbosim and Bein Ha'sdarim. Members of the Kollel are highly encouraged to begin studying the Mishnayos of the chosen mesechet in advance of the summer.

Packing Tips

  1. Label EVERYTHING with your child's name — clothing, shoes, bedding, toiletries, and equipment.
  2. Do NOT pack more than two pieces of luggage. Packing more than two bags is a common cause of bunk clutter.
  3. Pack clothing your child can manage independently — avoid items that require special care or are too difficult to fold.
  4. Please do not pack baby wipes — they often clog the toilets.

Notes on Nosh

Our first priority is the safety and health of our campers. Aside from Opening Day and Visiting Day, no package containing any food or drinks is to be sent to campers. All packages are directed to the Main Office, opened, and any food will be removed. No perishables, including meat, may be sent up with campers or staff.

We Are a Nut-Sensitive Camp

We have many campers who have severe allergies. The camp kitchen and canteen do not prepare or serve any food products that contain peanuts, tree nuts, or sesame, OR offer products labeled "made in a facility that processes nuts." Nosh brought to camp may not contain any such products.
