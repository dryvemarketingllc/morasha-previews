Design Notes

What changed: The photo shrinks from ~40% of the canvas to a deliberate 120px rounded rectangle. This does two things: it stops the empty circle from reading as "unfinished," and it puts the name first — which is what people actually scan for on social.

Quote added: A short pull quote below the name gives each announcement personality. Without it, you're just showing a face and a title — same as a company directory. The quote makes people pause.

Landscape as atmosphere: The illustrated mountains and lake stay, but pushed to the bottom third as ambient texture. They set the mood without competing with the person.

Photo note: The rounded rectangle works better than a circle for headshots — circles crop out shoulders and context, making everyone look like a LinkedIn avatar. The rectangle lets you show more of the person.

For production: Replace the "photo" placeholder with an actual headshot. The quote should come from the staff member — even one sentence works. The Playfair Display serif gives a more editorial feel than the current all-caps sans-serif treatment.
