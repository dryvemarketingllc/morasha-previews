Core Values

The seven values that spell Morasha.

Our programs are built around seven values that guide our campers toward a life of meaning, learning, and leadership.

M

Mutual Respect

ואהבת לרעך כמוך

A safe and nurturing environment where campers and Yachad members discover the Tzelem Elokim — the divine image — in others, and in themselves.

O

Observe & Study

ללמוד ולעשות

Campers LEARN Torah from passionate teachers and LIVE it in their bunks and on the ball fields — Torah, mitzvot, and Zionism woven into every day.

R

Responsibility

כל ישראל ערבים זה בזה

Hillel's words guide us: campers grow into dependable, conscientious members of a family, a community, a people, and a nation.

A

Appreciation

לשמע בקול תודה

Gratitude is our attitude — from morning tefilot to brachot in the Dining Room to the routine "thank you." Appreciation is the essence of a Yehudi.

S

Skill Building

יגיע כפיך כי תאכל

On the fields, courts, lake, stage, and beyond, campers build technical skill, confidence, and leadership through athletics, aquatics, adventure, and the arts.

H

Heart & Soul

בכל לבבך ובכל נפשך

Everything at Morasha — Color War, Shiria, zmirot, tefilla — is approached with passion and ruach, so spirits soar and souls are inspired.

A

Achdut

כאיש אחד בלב אחד

All for one and one for all. Bunks, teams, trips, and projects forge the bonds and affection that make everyone part of the Morasha family.
