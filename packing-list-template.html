Packing Tips

  1. Label EVERYTHING with your child's name — clothing, shoes, bedding, toiletries, and equipment.
  2. Do NOT pack more than two pieces of luggage. Packing more than two bags is a common cause of bunk clutter.
  3. Pack clothing your child can manage independently — avoid items that require special care or are too difficult to fold.
  4. Sleeping bags are needed for overnight trips. Check your camp's specific trip schedule to know when to send one.
  5. Please do not pack baby wipes — they often clog the toilets.

Notes on Nosh

Our first priority is the safety and health of our campers. Aside from Opening Day and Visiting Day, no package containing any food or drinks is to be sent to campers. This includes shipping or sending food with visitors or guests. All packages are directed to the Main Office, opened, and any food will be removed. No perishables, including meat, may be sent up with campers or staff.

A large fridge is placed on every bunk porch to store cold drinks only.

We Are a Nut-Sensitive Camp

We have many campers who have severe allergies. The camp kitchen and canteen do not prepare or serve any food products that contain peanuts, tree nuts, or sesame, OR offer products labeled "made in a facility that processes nuts." Nosh brought to camp may not contain any such products.
